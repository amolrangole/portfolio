01 — About

Pixel-perfect isn't a slogan. It's Tuesday.

I'm a Frontend/UI Developer with 6 years 10 months of professional experience building responsive, user-centric web applications and widgets across insurance and ecommerce. I work mainly in HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript (ES6+), and I'm currently building with React.js as I take on more component-driven work.

My day-to-day is converting Figma designs into pixel-perfect, cross-browser, mobile-first widgets — the forms and quote listings that sit on publisher sites and actually decide whether a visitor converts. I care about clean, reusable code, tight collaboration with design and QA, and shipping widgets that hold up across devices and A/B tests.
